TrackWerk

TrackWerk is by Kid Bunka, built on PocketTracker by conanizer and contributors. The application is GPL-3.0-or-later. The source package includes third-party notices and corresponding source for bundled components. Miyoo SDL has a separate exact source archive beside the release packages.

Components and lettering

TrackWerk uses open-source synthesis, audio, decoding and interface components. The complete source and bundled licences remain the authoritative inventory. Website fonts are Liberation Sans and Liberation Mono under the SIL Open Font License; their licence is in assets/fonts. The app’s optional bitmap typefaces retain the notices in its source.

Music

The Soul Index, Lofi Jazz and MPC Dust demonstration mixes are based on TrackWerk demo arrangements, re-rendered in TrackWerk 1.2.0 with recorded acoustic drum articulations. Melodic loops come from Kid Bunka’s DopeSONIX LoFi Jazz & Soul Vol 02 library. The compositions combine the loops with other sounds; commercial raw samples and sample-bearing project folders are excluded from the public application and demo project downloads.

DopeSONIX composition-use licence. Recorded drums: Virtuosity Drums, by Versilian Studios and Karoryfer Samples; performed by Austin McMahon; CC0-1.0 source and licence. Soul Index / Punch Reprise uses the same jazz/soul sample sources and recorded drum kit. Deep Organic uses internal TrackWerk synthesis. Arrangements and release-film editing used AI assistance; all musical playback and Punch processing demonstrated are native TrackWerk output.

Film and images

Actual 640 × 480 native interface captures are enlarged in the film. Navigation is scripted. Handheld shells are illustrations used to show the profiles, not camera footage or a claim that every depicted device was filmed during this session. Mabel narration is generated via ElevenLabs in Runway. The film includes English captions and chapter marks.

Release scope

Android, Brick/KNULLI, Miyoo Mini/Onion, Miyoo Mini Plus/Onion Archive and RGB20S/AmberELEC are distinct packages. Capabilities depend on the profile. Performance numbers should always identify the measured build and workload; this website makes no universal CPU or polyphony claim.
