NEW IN TRACKWERK 1.3.0

Type a lyric.
Play its voice.

Froggy sings the words you type. Five new synth models, clearer controls, MIDI export and a sample collection ready for your next beat.

Froggy sings your words

Choose Voices and Frogs > Froggy. Type lyrics on the on-screen keyboard or choose a template. Each note sings the next syllable. Shape four voice types with Mouth, Breath, Throat, Croak, Vibrato, Glide and Coda.

The frog stage follows the real sound, with a firefly XY control and karaoke words. Move it with L + D-pad or drag it on a touch screen. Motion is optional. Microphone pitch tracking is available in Linux builds.

Learn Froggy and its controls

Croaker

A croaking voice with throat size and a pitch dip. Shape a character, then play it as an instrument.

Acid Box

Cutoff, resonance and envelope modulation, with accent, decay, squash and a sub oscillator.

Reese Bass

Three detuned saws, a sub, filter movement and drift speed. Start with one of sixteen sounds.

Dub Siren

Sound-system calls with LFO rate, depth and sweep. Add the project delay and reverb for space.

128 models, easier to find

Synth families are grouped by purpose. One-line descriptions, named pages and control sheets with dials, LCDs or bar graphs make each instrument easier to explore. The factory sound browser, favourites, recent sounds and First Beat tutorial remain part of the workflow.

Explore the synth families

More ways to keep a good idea

MIDI export and preset inbox

EXPORT > MIDI writes a Standard MIDI File. Drop supported presets into Presets/Inbox and TrackWerk sorts readable files into the right folders without overwriting existing presets.

Probability and project backups

PRB gives a phrase row a chance to play on each pass. SAVE keeps the last five project versions in Backups and flushes saved data before completing.

Audio diagnosis and monitoring

Hold L1 + R1 for about a second to open AUDIO DRIVERS. Studio > Record includes input monitoring, contributed by @ozkaoz.

Your established studio

Eight tracks, Drumlab, Harmony, sample editing and slicing, archive discovery, Punch, mix recording, effects, mastering and mix/stem export. Forty Scenes and fifty-four palettes let you choose a readable workspace.

INCLUDED / TRACKWERK SAMPLE PACK 1.3

Open the browser.
Start a beat.

800 one-shots and breaks selected by Kid Bunka, plus 728 classic drum-machine sounds in their own folder. English and German PDF manuals start with a beginner quick start.

The main collection has 100 sounds each: kicks, snares, hi-hats, claps, bongos and congas, extras, toms and cymbals, and breaks. WAV, 16-bit, 44.1 kHz. The drum-machine collection keeps the recordings in its own library.

First Install packages include the libraries and both manuals. Existing installations can add the separate downloads. Android uses a signed APK plus a companion ZIP for samples and manuals. Follow the folder instructions in your package.

Use the samples in commercial or non-commercial music. Do not resell or redistribute them as a sample pack; read the included licence. The sample library licence is separate from the application's GPL licence.
