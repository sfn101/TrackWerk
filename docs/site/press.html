PRESS ROOM / TRACKWERK 1.3.0

A demo party
in your pocket.

TrackWerk 1.3.0 is a free, open-source eight-track handheld music studio by Kid Bunka. Forty complete visual Scenes meet sample editing, archive discovery, rhythm generation, synthesis and momentary performance effects.

What changed in 1.3

  • Froggy sings typed lyrics, with four voice types, lyric templates and a karaoke frog stage.
  • 128 synth models, including Croaker, Acid Box, Reese Bass and Dub Siren; sixteen starting sounds per new model.
  • Purpose-based synth categories, named pages and family-specific control sheets.
  • 800 selected one-shots and breaks plus 728 classic drum-machine sounds; English and German PDF manuals.
  • MIDI export, preset inbox, PRB row probability, five project backups on SAVE, audio-driver diagnosis and input monitoring.

Device programs tested by Kid Bunka: Android, Brick, Miyoo Mini/Mini Flip, Mini Plus and RGB20S. Other ports and new installation archives carry separate test status. Read the illustrated 1.3 guide.

Short description

Write a phrase, dig for a recording, chop a motif and perform the transition. TrackWerk brings an eight-track music workflow to Android and selected Linux handhelds, with a visual personality that can go from terminal green to daylight paper.

Which handheld
should play next?

Want TrackWerk on another handheld or operating system? Please open a GitHub issue with the exact model, firmware or OS version, processor and the controls or audio support you can test. Testers and contributors are welcome.

TrackWerk is free and open source. If you would like to help with continued development, testing hardware and new instruments, optional donations are welcome. You can also help by reporting a reproducible problem or sharing music you made.
